# Brag Plan: Krono

## What is this app?
Krono is an open-source Android app that puts every competitive-programming contest, your ratings across platforms and a head-to-head with your rivals on one clock.

## The angle
Quiet premium product film for the new "pebble" identity: cream and navy, chunky outlines, butter/sky/mint/coral fills. The app's own screens carry the video; copy is lifted from the README.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Butter "K" tile pops in, "Krono" and the README tagline: "Competitive programming, on the clock." Three pills: Contests, Ratings, Rivals.

## Key moments
- Contests: dark Contests screen, "One countdown for every contest."
- Today: light Today screen, "your ratings across platforms."
- Rivals: dark Rivals screen, "a head-to-head with your rivals."

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup, "Competitive programming, on the clock.", "Open source · MeetThakur/Krono".

## User flow worth showing
Check the next contest countdown -> see profiles and daily goal -> compare against a rival.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: friendly premium product film, chunky and warm
- Interpretation: confident holds, snappy 0.4s entrances, no jokes.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18.0 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #FFF8E7, ink #1D2233, butter #FFD66B, sky #BFE3FA, mint #C9F0DA, coral #FFB3A0
- Display font: Fredoka. Body font: Nunito.
- Strongest visual element: the outlined pastel cards and the K tile.

## Share copy (draft)
Krono 2.0: every contest on one clock, your ratings across platforms, a head-to-head with your rivals. Open source Android.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (bgm.mp3), ~110 BPM, quiet under the outro
- Music cue guidance: scene cuts land on beats of the ~0.545s grid (3.27s, 7.63s, 11.99s, 15.26s)
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (extraction helper not used in this run)
- SFX posture: sparse; click on cards, soft chime on the headline reveals, bell on the outro
- Restraint rule: music stays under the visuals
