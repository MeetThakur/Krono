# Brag Plan: Krono

## What is this app?
Krono is a high-performance native Android competitive programming companion built with Jetpack Compose that tracks multi-platform contests across Codeforces, LeetCode, and CodeChef, drives a daily practice streak engine, and ranks you against friends on a live rivals leaderboard.

## The angle
Competitive programmers juggle three different platforms, constantly missing rounds or losing practice momentum. Krono unifies every arena into a single, razor-sharp dark Material 3 interface that turns everyday contest tracking and problem solving into an addictive daily habit.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-energy opening with the glowing Krono logo and the line:
**"Never miss a round. Never lose a streak."**
Instantly followed by the three platform badges: Codeforces, LeetCode, CodeChef.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Consolidated Contest Radar**: Live, upcoming, and past rounds in a timezone-adjusted timeline with smart alarm alerts.
- **Daily Practice Engine**: Real-time streak tracking, Problem of the Day (POTD), and custom goal targets (1, 2, 3, 5, or custom stepper).
- **Rivals Leaderboard**: Live side-by-side rating comparisons and rank cards to outpace your peers.

## Outro / punchline
- **"100% Native. 100% Kotlin. Built with Jetpack Compose."**
- Final logo lockup with GitHub repository `MeetThakur/Krono` and "Made with ❤️ by Meet".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Checking today's daily streak target and connected platform ratings.
2. **Key Action 1**: Browsing upcoming contests across Codeforces, LeetCode, and CodeChef with 1-tap reminders.
3. **Key Action 2**: Comparing your rating against your top rivals on the leaderboard.
4. **Result**: Motivation to maintain your streak and crush the next contest.
